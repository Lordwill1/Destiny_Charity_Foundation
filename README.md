## About Destiny Toby Charity Foundation

The Destiny Toby Charity Foundation was founded by Rev. Fr. Ben Ubanii, Ikenna Ubanii, Odinakachi Emma-Osuji, and Dr. Ogechi Bethel-Eke, who also serves as the Director.

At Destiny Toby Charity, we are a passionate and dedicated team committed to transforming lives and creating brighter futures for underprivileged communities. Guided by the values of compassion, integrity, and empowerment, we believe that every person deserves the chance to reach their full potential.

Our mission goes beyond temporary relief, we aim to address the root causes of inequality, providing resources, opportunities, and support that empower individuals and uplift entire communities.

Together, we work tirelessly to bring hope, inspire change, and build a fairer, more inclusive world for all.
